Why Pathwork

Built around finishing, not just starting.

Plenty of platforms make it easy to start a course. Pathwork is built around the harder part: actually finishing, and having something to show for it.

  • Structured learning paths

    Courses are ordered for you, so you always know the next step instead of choosing at random.

  • Practical projects

    Each path ends in projects you build yourself, so you leave with proof of what you can do.

  • Progress tracking

    See exactly how far you are through a course or path, and pick up right where you left off.

  • Industry-relevant skills

    Course content is scoped around what's actually used on the job, not just theory.

  • Recognizable certificates

    Finish a path and get a certificate you can add to your resume or profile.

  • Learn at your own pace

    No deadlines or cohorts. Pause a path for a month and pick it back up without losing progress.
